How It Works

This interactive traffic light simulation demonstrates a manual traffic signal control:

  • Red Light: Click the switch button to start the transition. Yellow appears for 1 second before switching.
  • Yellow Light: Transition light that appears for 1 second between red and green.
  • Green Light: Click again to switch back to red (via yellow transition).

The sequence alternates between red and green with a yellow transition. Both vertical and horizontal traffic lights operate in perfect sync.

💡 Technical Note: The timing and transitions are controlled by JavaScript, with a configuration system that manages state changes and visual feedback.
